# Architecture Review Board

## Purpose

The architecture review board provides a decision forum for architecture standards, exceptions, target-state alignment, and modernization priorities.

## Board Responsibilities

- review significant design decisions
- approve or reject exceptions
- maintain standards alignment
- remove blockers from modernization work
- keep decisions documented and traceable

## Board Outcome

The board should make architecture decisions faster and more consistent, not simply add another approval layer.

## Meeting Outputs

- decisions
- actions
- exceptions
- follow-up items
