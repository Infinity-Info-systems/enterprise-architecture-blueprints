# Business Capability Map

## Purpose

Map business capabilities so architecture decisions can align to outcomes rather than systems alone.

## Capability Areas

- customer engagement
- operations
- compliance
- finance
- service delivery
- resilience
- growth

## How To Use

- identify critical capabilities
- align modernization work to those capabilities
- use the map for prioritization and sequencing

## Use

Use this map to keep architecture decisions tied to the business capabilities that matter most.
