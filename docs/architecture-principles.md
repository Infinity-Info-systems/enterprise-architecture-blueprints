# Architecture Principles

## Purpose

Architecture principles establish the design rules that shape decisions across platforms, services, and transformation programs.

## Example Principles

- prefer standard patterns over one-off solutions
- design for security and resilience by default
- keep ownership clear
- reduce integration complexity where possible
- align architecture to measurable business outcomes
- prefer reusable patterns over bespoke designs

## How To Use Them

- use principles during design review
- use principles to evaluate exceptions
- use principles to align modernization roadmaps

## Evidence To Capture

- documented principle statements
- review decisions
- exception approvals
- design outcomes

## Principle Outcome

Principles should be concrete enough to guide design choices and consistent enough to support governance.
