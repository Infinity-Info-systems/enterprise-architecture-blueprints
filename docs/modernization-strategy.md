# Modernization Strategy

## Purpose

The modernization strategy explains how architecture changes are sequenced, funded, and delivered.

## Strategy Themes

- standardization
- consolidation
- platform modernization
- integration simplification
- governance improvement
- architecture reuse

## Prioritization Criteria

- business value
- risk reduction
- dependency path
- implementation effort
- strategic fit

## Outputs

- modernization themes
- priority sequence
- decision points
- roadmap checkpoints

## Strategy Rule

Modernization should reduce complexity and increase reuse, not just move systems from one place to another.
# Modernization Strategy

## Purpose

This page describes how the organization should move from legacy architecture patterns to the target state.

## Strategy Themes

- reduce unnecessary complexity
- sequence work by dependency and value
- modernize the highest-risk areas first
- protect critical services during transition
- keep standards aligned with delivery reality

## Use

Use this page when the team needs to explain the order of modernization work and the rationale behind it.

## Outcome

A good modernization strategy makes the transition achievable instead of aspirational.
