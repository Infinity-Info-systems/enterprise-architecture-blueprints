# Modernization Strategy

## Purpose

The modernization strategy explains how architecture changes are sequenced, funded, and delivered.

## Strategy Themes

- standardization
- consolidation
- platform modernization
- integration simplification
- governance improvement
- architecture reuse

## Prioritization Criteria

- business value
- risk reduction
- dependency path
- implementation effort
- strategic fit

## Outputs

- modernization themes
- priority sequence
- decision points
- roadmap checkpoints

## Strategy Rule

Modernization should reduce complexity and increase reuse, not just move systems from one place to another.
